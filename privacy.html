Privacy

There is no account to make, nothing to sign up for, and no analytics. The music is composed on your device and never leaves it. This page is short because there is very little to tell you.

What is collected

Nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no cookies. Nothing you type into the three word fields is sent anywhere — the words are read by code running in your browser and used to choose a key, a tempo and a set of instruments.

What is stored on your device

One value, in your browser’s local storage: whether you chose the day or the night theme. It stays on your device, is readable only by this site, and is removed if you clear your browsing data. Nothing else is stored, and no cookies are set.

The music

Every note is generated in your browser and written to audio there. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is recorded, and the microphone is never requested. Nothing is sampled from anyone else’s recordings either — the instruments are synthesised from scratch, so what you hear has no copyright owner but the code that made it.

Other companies

None, while the page is running. The typefaces are served from this site rather than from a font CDN, specifically so that loading the page does not hand your IP address to a third party.

The files themselves are hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub’s servers see the request that fetches them — including your IP address — in the same way any web server sees any request for a file. That is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Sleep mode

Typing sleep gives you a slower, quieter piece tuned to A = 432 Hz with two low tones a few hertz apart, one to each ear. That is a real and audible effect, and it is what this kind of music is conventionally made of.

It is not a medical or therapeutic claim. The evidence that tones like these change anything in the listener is weak, and nothing here is offered as a treatment for anything. It is music to fall asleep to, in the same sense that any quiet record is.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes — if anything is ever measured, stored or sent — it will say so here first. Questions, or a correction to anything on this page, are welcome at the project’s issue tracker.
